Change your engine oil according to the recommended service schedule.

RIDE SMART, RIDE SAFE

ROAD SAFETY REMINDERS

Always Wear a Helmet

Wear a properly fitted and approved motorcycle helmet every time you ride.

Follow Traffic Rules

Respect traffic signs, signals, and speed limits.

Avoid Distractions

Keep your attention on the road and never use your phone while riding.

Wear Protective Gear

Use proper clothing, gloves, and shoes for better protection.

ABOUT OUR WEBSITE

WHAT IS MOTOGUIDE PH?

MotoGuide PH is an informational website created to help motorcycle enthusiasts and beginners learn more about motorcycles. It provides basic information about motorcycle models, maintenance tips, and road safety reminders. Our goal is to promote knowledge and responsible riding.
